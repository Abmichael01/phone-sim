Lagos

Article summary:Lagos is the largest city in Nigeria and one of the largest in Africa, with a population estimated at over 15 million people.[1] It is a major financial centre and was the national capital until 1991.[2]

History

The area was settled by the Awori, a subgroup of the Yoruba people, in the 15th century.[3] Portuguese explorers named it Lago de Curamo, which later became Lagos.[4][5]

Geography

The city spreads over islands and the mainland around the Lagos Lagoon, joined by long bridges.[6]
